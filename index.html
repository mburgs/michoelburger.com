About

The short version

I've been building software for over fifteen years, mostly in healthcare. I was a founding engineer at Hospital IQ, managed engineering teams at Regard, and now lead engineering at Neuroflow, a behavioral health platform. A lot of that work has been EHR integrations, data infrastructure, and figuring out how to get useful clinical tools into the hands of people who actually need them.

Somewhere along the way I noticed that the parts of my job I cared about most weren't really about code. They were about sitting across from someone on my team who was going through something hard and being useful in that conversation. I kept gravitating toward the people stuff — mentoring, hiring, helping folks navigate rough patches — and eventually I figured I should get proper training.

So now I'm also getting my MS in Mental Health Counseling at NYU Steinhardt. I don't plan to pick one career over the other. I like building things and I like talking to people, and I'm lucky that both of those can be the job.
